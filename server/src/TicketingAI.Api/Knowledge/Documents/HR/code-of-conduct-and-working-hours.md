# Code of Conduct and Working Hours

## Working hours
Standard working hours are 09:30 to 17:30, Sunday to Friday, with a one-hour lunch break. Saturday is the weekly day off. This is 42 working hours a week, within the 48-hour weekly limit of the Labour Act. Flexible start times are possible within the core hours rules in the remote work policy.

## Attendance and lateness
Arriving more than 30 minutes late on a regular basis should be discussed with your manager. Repeated unexplained lateness can lead to a formal conversation. If you will be late or absent, let your manager know as early as possible.

## Dress code
The dress code is smart casual. Employees who meet clients wear business attire on those days. Company-branded clothing is provided to some teams for events.

## Respectful workplace
The company does not tolerate harassment, discrimination or bullying of any kind. This covers behavior in the office, online, at events and in messages. Everyone is expected to treat colleagues, clients and vendors with respect. Sexual harassment at the workplace is also prohibited by Nepali law.

## Reporting a concern
Report harassment or other conduct concerns to your manager, to HR, or confidentially at hr-confidential@ticket.com. Reports are handled in confidence, and the company does not allow retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith. HR acknowledges a report within 2 working days.

## Conflicts of interest
Employees must tell HR about any outside work, business or financial interest that could conflict with their role. Taking a second job in the same industry needs written approval. Accepting gifts worth more than NPR 5,000 from vendors or clients is not allowed.

## Disciplinary process
Minor issues are usually handled with a verbal warning, followed by a written warning if the problem continues. Serious misconduct can lead to immediate suspension while it is investigated. Employees always get the chance to explain their side before a decision is made.

## Resignation and notice period
After probation, the notice period is 30 days for most employees and 60 days for managers. Submit your resignation in writing to your manager and HR. Unused annual leave can be taken during the notice period, and accrued unused annual leave is paid out in the final salary.

## Exit process
In your last week, you return your laptop, access card and any other company items, and IT closes your accounts on your last day. HR holds an exit interview and gives you a relieving letter and experience letter once all items are returned.
