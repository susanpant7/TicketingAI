# Health Insurance and Benefits

## Medical insurance coverage
All full-time employees are covered by company-paid medical insurance from their first day. Coverage extends to your spouse and up to 2 dependent children at no cost to you. The annual coverage limit is NPR 500,000 (5 lakh) per family.

## Adding or removing dependents
You can add a spouse or child within 30 days of marriage or birth by sending proof to hr@ticket.com. Outside those events, dependents can only be changed during the annual enrolment window in Poush. Additional dependents beyond the 2 children can be added at your own cost.

## Cashless treatment and claims
At network hospitals you can use your insurance card for cashless treatment, and the insurer settles the bill directly. At other hospitals you pay first and submit a reimbursement claim with bills and the discharge summary to HR within 30 days. Reimbursements usually take 3 to 4 weeks.

## What is not covered
Cosmetic treatment, treatment outside Nepal, and conditions that existed before joining are not covered during the first 12 months. Routine dental and vision care are not part of the standard plan.

## Dental and vision add-on
An optional dental and vision plan is available for NPR 2,000 per month, deducted from salary. Enrol in the HR portal in Poush or within 30 days of joining.

## Wellness allowance
Each employee gets a wellness allowance of NPR 2,500 per month, which can be used for a gym membership, sports activities or mental health apps. Claim it quarterly as an expense with receipts.

## Learning and development budget
Each employee has a learning budget of NPR 60,000 per year for courses, certifications and books related to their work. Get your manager's approval before you pay, and submit the receipt as an expense. Unused budget does not carry over.

## Retirement savings
The company enrols all employees in the Social Security Fund (SSF). 11 percent of basic salary is contributed by the employee and 20 percent by the company. See the Social Security Fund and Gratuity document for how it works, how to check your balance and how to withdraw.
