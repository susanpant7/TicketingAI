# Onboarding and Probation

## First day
On your first day, arrive at 09:30 at reception. HR will welcome you, check your identity documents, and sign your employment paperwork. IT will hand over your laptop and accounts, and you will meet your manager and your onboarding buddy.

## Documents to bring
Bring your citizenship certificate or passport, your PAN card, your educational certificates, your previous employer's relieving letter, your SSF number if you already have one, and your bank account details for salary. Passport-size photographs are needed for your access card. Missing documents can be submitted within the first 2 weeks.

## Onboarding buddy
Every new joiner is paired with a buddy from their team for the first 3 months. The buddy answers day-to-day questions, introduces you to people and tools, and checks in with you weekly. Your buddy is named in your welcome email.

## First week and training
In the first week you will complete mandatory training on information security, the code of conduct and workplace safety. These courses are on the learning platform and take about 3 hours in total. Your manager also shares a 30-60-90 day plan for your role.

## Probation period
New employees serve a probation period of 3 months. During probation, either side can end the employment with 1 week of notice. Annual leave accrues during probation, but remote work days are only available after it ends.

## Probation reviews
Your manager reviews your progress with you at 30, 60 and 90 days. At the 90-day review you will be told in writing whether you are confirmed. In some cases probation can be extended once by up to 3 months, and you will be told the reasons.

## Getting help in the first weeks
For IT problems with your laptop or accounts, contact IT support. For questions about leave, payroll or benefits, contact HR at hr@ticket.com. For anything about your role, ask your manager or your buddy.
