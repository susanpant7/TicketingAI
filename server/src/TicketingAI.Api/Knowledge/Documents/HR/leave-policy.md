## Annual leave entitlement
Every full-time employee gets 18 days of paid annual leave per year, called home leave in Nepali labour law. This follows the Labour Act accrual of 1 day for every 20 days worked. Annual leave accrues monthly, and new joiners accrue from their start date.

## Sick leave
Employees get 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate from a registered doctor is required when sick leave lasts more than 3 consecutive working days. Tell your manager as early as possible on the first day of absence, ideally before 10:00.

## Casual leave
Employees get 5 days of casual leave per year for short personal matters. Casual leave can be taken for a single day at a time, needs no reason beyond manager approval, and cannot be combined with annual leave for a stretch of more than 10 consecutive days.

## How to apply for leave
Apply for leave in the HR portal under Leave > New Request. Give at least 3 working days notice for 1 to 2 days of leave, and at least 2 weeks notice for more than 5 days. Your manager approves or rejects the request in the portal, usually within 2 working days. Sick leave can be recorded after the fact.

## Carry forward of unused leave
Unused annual leave is not lost at the end of the year. It accumulates up to a maximum of 90 days, and unused sick leave accumulates up to 45 days. Casual leave does not carry over. Accumulated annual leave is paid out in cash when you leave the company, calculated on your basic salary.

## Public holidays
The company observes at least 13 public holidays per year, following the government holiday list, including the Dashain and Tihar holidays. The full list is published in the HR portal each Baisakh. If a public holiday falls on Saturday, the weekly day off, it is not moved to another day. See the public holidays and festivals document for details.

## Parental leave
Mothers get 98 days of fully paid maternity leave. Fathers get 15 days of paid paternity leave, which must be taken within 3 months of the birth or adoption. Tell HR at least 8 weeks before the expected date, if possible, so cover can be arranged.

## Bereavement leave
Employees get 13 days of paid mourning leave (the kaaj kriya period) on the death of a spouse, child, parent, sibling or grandparent. Inform your manager and HR as soon as you can. No advance application is needed.

## Unpaid leave
Unpaid leave of up to 30 days in a year can be requested after annual leave is used up. It needs approval from both your manager and HR, and it can affect benefits such as health insurance if it exceeds 30 days.
