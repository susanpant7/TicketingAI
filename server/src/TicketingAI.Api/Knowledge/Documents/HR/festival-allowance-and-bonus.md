## Dashain allowance
Employees receive a Dashain allowance equal to one month of basic salary. It is paid before the Dashain holidays begin, usually in the month of Ashwin, together with or just before the salary. Payroll publishes the exact payment date in the HR portal each year.

## Who is eligible
Employees who have completed their probation and are still employed on the payment date are eligible. The full allowance is paid to employees who worked the full fiscal year. For employees who joined during the fiscal year, the allowance is pro-rated by the number of months worked since joining.

## Employees on leave or notice
Employees on paid leave, including sick and maternity leave, still receive the allowance. Employees on unpaid leave receive it pro-rated for the paid months. Employees who have resigned and are serving a notice period on the payment date still receive it.

## Statutory profit bonus
Where the company makes a profit, a statutory bonus is shared among employees as required by the Bonus Act. It is paid after the annual accounts for the fiscal year are finalised, and the amount depends on the company's profit for that year. Payroll announces the amount and date once it is known.

## Tax on allowance and bonus
Income tax (TDS) is deducted from the Dashain allowance and the bonus, as it is part of your taxable income. The payslip shows the allowance on its own line, and the tax effect is calculated for the whole year. Contact payroll@ticket.com if you have questions about the calculation.

## Festival gifts
The company may give a small festival gift, such as sweets or a gift card, to employees during Dashain and Tihar. These gifts are in addition to the allowance, and have no effect on salary or tax.
