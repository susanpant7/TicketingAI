# Emergencies and Business Continuity

## Emergency numbers
In an emergency in Nepal, call the police on 100, the fire brigade on 101 and an ambulance on 102. The tourist police can be reached on 1144. Save these numbers, and the HR emergency line, which is on the back of your access card.

## Earthquakes
Nepal is in an earthquake zone, so everyone should know what to do. If the ground shakes, drop to the floor, take cover under a sturdy desk, and hold on until the shaking stops. Do not run outside or use the lifts. After the shaking stops, leave the building by the stairs and go to the assembly point shown on the floor plan.

## After an earthquake
Wait at the assembly point until the floor warden says it is safe to re-enter. Employees must mark themselves as safe in the HR portal or by messaging their manager. If the building is damaged, the company will arrange for everyone to work from home until it has been inspected.

## Bandhs, strikes and road blockades
During a bandh, chakka jam or strike, when roads are blocked or travel is unsafe, employees work from home. Managers confirm by 08:00 whether the office is open. No leave is deducted for days when the office is closed for these reasons, and employees are expected to do the work they can from home.

## Monsoon floods and landslides
During the monsoon, heavy rain can cause floods, landslides and road blockages. If your journey to the office or your home area is unsafe, tell your manager and work from home. Do not take risks to reach the office.

## Fuel shortages and transport disruptions
When fuel shortages or transport strikes make commuting difficult, managers can allow remote work for the affected days. The company also arranges office shuttles on the main routes when the disruption continues for more than 2 days.

## Fire safety
Know where the fire exits and extinguishers are on your floor. If the fire alarm sounds, leave the building by the stairs and gather at the assembly point. Fire drills are held twice a year, and all employees must take part.

## Staying in touch
Keep your phone number and emergency contact up to date in the HR portal. In a major emergency, HR sends updates by SMS and email, and updates the status on the intranet.
