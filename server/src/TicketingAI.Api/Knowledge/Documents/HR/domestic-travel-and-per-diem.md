# Domestic Travel Policy

## Approval and booking
Business travel inside Nepal needs your manager's approval before you book. Submit a travel request in the HR portal with the destination, dates and purpose. Book through the travel desk where possible, so that tickets and hotels are booked at company rates.

## Hotel limits by location
Hotels are reimbursed up to NPR 8,000 per night in the Kathmandu Valley, NPR 5,000 per night in other cities such as Pokhara, Biratnagar, Butwal and Nepalgunj, and NPR 3,000 per night in smaller towns and rural areas. Stays above the limit need your department head's approval before the trip.

## Flights and road travel
Use economy class on domestic flights. Flights are preferred for long distances to places such as Pokhara, Biratnagar, Bhairahawa and the Karnali region, because road journeys are long and can be unsafe in the monsoon. For shorter trips, tourist buses, micro-buses and company-approved taxis can be used with receipts.

## Using your own motorcycle or car
If your manager approves the use of your own vehicle for business, the company pays NPR 10 per km for a motorcycle and NPR 25 per km for a car, on the distance for the approved trip. Fuel, parking and tolls are included in this rate. You must carry a valid licence, and the vehicle must be insured.

## Meals and daily costs
The meal allowance from the expense policy, NPR 2,000 per day, applies when you travel for business. There is no separate daily allowance on top of it. Local transport at the destination is reimbursed with receipts.

## Safety on the road
Avoid night travel on highways, especially in the hills, and do not travel on roads closed by landslides or flooding. During the monsoon from Jestha to Ashwin, check the weather and road conditions before you leave. If your manager asks you to travel when it is not safe, you can refuse and tell HR.

## Travel insurance and emergencies
Employees on business travel are covered by the company's accident insurance. Keep the emergency numbers with you, and call HR or your manager if there is an accident or a disruption. For flight cancellations, rebook through the travel desk, and tell your manager.

## Claiming travel costs
After the trip, submit an expense claim within 30 days with your tickets, hotel bills and receipts. Settle any cash advance in the same claim. Expenses above the limits in this policy are not reimbursed unless they were approved in advance.
