## Salary payment date
Salaries are paid on the last working day of each Nepali (BS) month by bank transfer. If the last day of the month is a Saturday or public holiday, salary is paid on the working day before it. All dates in HR and payroll policies, such as the 15th or the 20th, refer to BS calendar months unless stated otherwise.

## Payslips
Payslips are available in the HR portal under Payroll > Payslips on the day salary is paid. Each payslip shows gross pay, deductions such as income tax (TDS) and Social Security Fund contributions, and net pay. Payslips for the last 24 months stay available online.

## Changing bank details
To change the bank account your salary is paid into, submit a bank details change request in the HR portal with a copy of a bank statement or cancelled cheque. Requests must be received by the 15th of the month to take effect in that month's salary. Requests after the 15th apply from the following month.

## Tax and deductions
Income tax is deducted from salary each month as TDS, based on your PAN and your tax declaration. Update your tax declaration in the HR portal by the end of Poush each year to claim eligible deductions. Questions about the calculation can be sent to payroll@ticket.com.

## Overtime and extra work
Non-managerial employees are paid 1.5 times the normal hourly rate for approved overtime, in line with the Labour Act. Overtime is limited to 4 hours a day and 20 hours a week. Managers and employees in managerial roles are not paid overtime. Overtime must be approved by your manager before it is worked.

## Salary review
Salaries are reviewed once a year in Baisakh, based on performance and company results. Revised salaries take effect from 1 Jestha. Promotions can also be considered outside this cycle when a role changes.

## Salary queries and errors
If your salary is wrong or missing, email payroll@ticket.com with your name, employee ID and a description of the issue. Payroll replies within 2 working days, and confirmed underpayments are corrected in an extra payment within 5 working days.
