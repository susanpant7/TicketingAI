title: Income Tax, TDS and PAN for Employees
department: HR
---

## PAN is required
Every employee needs a Permanent Account Number (PAN) from the Inland Revenue Department (IRD). Give your PAN to HR on your first day. Without a PAN, tax is deducted at a higher rate and you cannot claim deductions. If you do not have a PAN yet, ask HR to guide you through applying for one.

## How tax is deducted
The company deducts income tax from your salary every month as TDS (tax deducted at source) and deposits it with the IRD. The tax is calculated on your annual taxable income using the slabs in the current Finance Act, and payroll applies the rates announced for each fiscal year. The slabs change from year to year.

## Individual or couple assessment
Where the current rules allow it, a married employee can choose to be assessed as an individual or as a couple. Tell payroll your choice by the end of Poush, and send the supporting documents. The choice applies to the whole fiscal year.

## Deductions you can claim
Contributions to SSF are deducted automatically. You can also declare life and health insurance premiums, contributions to retirement funds such as CIT, and other deductions allowed under the current rules. Send proof to payroll through the HR portal by the end of Poush, and do not wait until the end of the fiscal year.

## Tax certificate
After the end of the fiscal year in Ashadh, payroll gives you a tax deduction certificate showing your income and the tax deducted. Keep it, because you may need it for loans, visas and your own tax filing. You can download it in the HR portal under Payroll > Tax.

## Income from other sources
If you have income from another source, such as rent or a second job, you may have to declare and pay tax on it separately. The company only deducts tax on the salary and benefits it pays you. Ask a tax adviser or the IRD if you are not sure.

## Questions about tax
Questions about your tax calculation can be sent to payroll@ticket.com with your employee ID and your payslip. Payroll replies within 2 working days, and can show you the calculation step by step.
