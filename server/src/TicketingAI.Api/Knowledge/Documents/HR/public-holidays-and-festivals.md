## How the holiday list works
The company follows the Nepal government public holiday list, which has at least 13 holidays a year. Many festival dates follow the lunar calendar, so they change every year. HR publishes the full list in the HR portal each Baisakh, using Bikram Sambat (BS) dates with the matching English dates. Saturday is the weekly day off, so holidays that fall on Saturday are not moved.

## Dashain
Dashain is the biggest festival of the year, and the company announces its Dashain break every year. The break is typically about a week and covers Ghatasthapana, Fulpati, Maha Ashtami, Maha Navami, Vijaya Dashami and the days after, when employees receive tika from elders. Travel home before the break is common, so apply for any extra leave around it early.

## Tihar
Tihar is the festival of lights, and the company holiday typically covers Laxmi Puja, Govardhan Puja and Bhai Tika, which is about 3 to 4 days. Mha Puja is observed by Newar employees and is a local holiday for the Kathmandu Valley.

## Other national holidays
Other public holidays include Nepali New Year (Baisakh 1), Buddha Jayanti, Republic Day, Janai Purnima, Krishna Janmashtami, Constitution Day, Maha Shivaratri, Fagu Purnima (Holi) and Maghe Sankranti. The government can add or remove days, and the HR portal shows the final list.

## Holidays for women employees
Women employees get a holiday on Haritalika Teej, as in the government list. This day is an additional day off and does not use annual leave.

## Valley and community festivals
Gai Jatra, Indra Jatra, Ghode Jatra and Sonam Lhosar are observed as holidays in the Kathmandu Valley or for the communities that celebrate them. Employees who celebrate a festival that is not on the list, such as Chhath or Eid, can use annual leave or casual leave for it.

## Working on a public holiday
Employees are not expected to work on public holidays. If an employee is asked to work on a public holiday for a business need, they receive compensatory time off within 30 days or pay at the overtime rate, as agreed with their manager before the work.
