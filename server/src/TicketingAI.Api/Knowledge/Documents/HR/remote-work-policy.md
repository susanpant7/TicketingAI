## Hybrid work model
The company follows a hybrid model. Employees work from the office at least 3 days a week and may work remotely on the other days. Teams agree their in-office days together, so that everyone is in on the same days at least twice a week.

## Eligibility
Remote work days are available after the 3-month probation period is completed. During probation, employees are expected to work from the office every day to learn the team and tools. Some roles, such as IT support on-site and reception, are office-based and not eligible for remote days.

## Core hours
Whether in the office or at home, employees must be available between 10:00 and 16:00 on working days. Outside core hours you may organize your day flexibly, as long as you complete your working hours and agreed work.

## Working from another city or country
Working remotely from outside the Kathmandu Valley, for example from your home district, for up to 2 weeks per year needs your manager's approval. Make sure you have a reliable internet connection and a power backup there. Working from another country is not permitted without written approval from HR, because of tax and legal rules.

## Equipment and home office
The company provides a laptop and, on request, a monitor and keyboard for the office. A one-time home office allowance of NPR 25,000 is available after probation for a desk, chair or similar. Submit receipts as described in the expense reimbursement policy.

## Security when working remotely
Always connect to the company VPN when accessing internal systems from outside the office. Do not work on company data from public computers, and lock your screen when you step away. Report a lost or stolen laptop to IT immediately.

## Internet and phone costs
The company reimburses up to NPR 1,500 per month toward home internet for employees who work remotely at least 2 days a week. Mobile phone costs are not reimbursed unless the phone is required for your role.
