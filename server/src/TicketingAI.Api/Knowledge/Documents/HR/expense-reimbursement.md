# Expense Reimbursement

## What can be reimbursed
The company reimburses reasonable business expenses that were approved in advance, such as travel, accommodation, client meals, and equipment or software needed for your work. Personal expenses and fines are never reimbursed.

## Travel and accommodation limits
Hotel stays are reimbursed up to NPR 8,000 per night in the Kathmandu Valley, and at the lower limits in the domestic travel policy elsewhere. Economy class is used for flights and a standard seat on tourist buses or other road transport. Local transport such as taxis is reimbursed when public transport is not practical, with a receipt. Travel must be booked through the travel desk where possible.

## Meal allowance
While travelling for business, meals are reimbursed up to NPR 2,000 per day. Client meals are reimbursed up to NPR 3,500 per person, and the names of attendees must be written on the claim. Alcohol is not reimbursed.

## Receipts
A receipt or invoice is required for every expense over NPR 1,000, and it should be a proper VAT or PAN bill showing the vendor's PAN. For smaller amounts, a short description is enough. If you lose a receipt, fill in the missing receipt declaration and ask your manager to approve it.

## How to submit a claim
Submit expense claims in the HR portal under Expenses > New Claim. Attach a photo or scan of each receipt, choose a category, and send the claim to your manager. Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense, or they can be rejected.

## Approval and payment
The manager approves the claim first, and then the finance team checks it. Claims approved before the 20th of the month are paid with that month's salary, on the last working day. Claims approved after the 20th are paid with the following month's salary.

## Cash advances
Employees travelling for more than 3 days can request a cash advance of up to 70 percent of the estimated travel cost. Ask your manager and finance at least 5 working days before the trip. The advance must be settled with receipts within 10 days after returning.
