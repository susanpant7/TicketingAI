## Standard laptop
Every full-time employee gets a company laptop on their first day. Standard laptops are Windows laptops, and developers can request a MacBook. All laptops have encrypted disks, endpoint protection and automatic updates enabled, and these settings must not be turned off.

## Requesting a new laptop or replacement
Laptops are replaced every 3 years. To request a replacement early, or a new laptop for a new joiner, raise a hardware request ticket with your manager's approval. Standard laptops are delivered within 5 working days, and special configurations can take up to 3 weeks.

## Laptop is broken or very slow
For hardware faults such as a broken screen, keyboard or battery, raise a ticket and bring the laptop to the IT desk. Repairs usually take 2 to 3 working days, and IT gives you a loan laptop in the meantime. For a slow laptop, restart it first and check that there are no pending updates, then raise a ticket if it still struggles.

## Lost or stolen laptop
Report a lost or stolen laptop to IT immediately by phone on extension 100 or by email to it-help@ticket.com, and do not wait until the next working day. IT will lock the device remotely and revoke your accounts. If it was stolen, also file a police report and give a copy to IT.

## Monitors, keyboards and accessories
On request, IT provides an external monitor, keyboard, mouse and headset for your desk. Raise an equipment ticket and collect the items from the IT desk. Accessories for home offices are covered by the home office allowance in the remote work policy.

## Returning equipment
Return your laptop, charger, access card and accessories to IT on your last working day or before you start a long absence. IT checks the equipment and wipes the laptop. HR will not release your relieving letter until the equipment is returned.

## Personal devices
Personal laptops cannot access company systems. Personal phones may be used for email, calendar and chat only after enrolling in mobile device management, which lets IT wipe company data if the phone is lost.
