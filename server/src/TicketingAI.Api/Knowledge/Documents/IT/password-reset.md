department: IT

## Password rules
Your company password must be at least 12 characters long and contain upper-case and lower-case letters, a number and a symbol. You cannot reuse any of your last 10 passwords. Passwords expire every 90 days, and you get a reminder email 14 days before expiry.

## Resetting a forgotten password yourself
Go to the self-service password reset page at password.ticket.com and choose "Forgot my password". Enter your company email, verify your identity with the multi-factor prompt on your phone or with a code sent to your registered personal email, and set a new password. The new password works for all company systems after about 5 minutes.

## Changing your password before it expires
Sign in to password.ticket.com, choose "Change password", and enter your old and new password. On a company laptop you can also press Ctrl+Alt+Delete and choose "Change a password". After changing it, update the password saved on your phone's email app, otherwise your account may lock.

## Account locked after failed attempts
Your account is locked for 15 minutes after 5 wrong password attempts. Wait 15 minutes and try again with the right password. A common cause is an old password stored on your phone or a mapped drive that keeps retrying, so update it everywhere.

## Cannot use self-service reset
If you cannot reach the multi-factor prompt or do not have a registered personal email, contact IT support. IT will verify your identity by calling your manager or by a video call, and then issue a temporary password. You must change the temporary password at first sign-in.

## Password security
Never share your password, even with IT. IT will never ask for your password by email or chat. Use a password manager approved by the company, and do not write passwords on paper near your desk. Report any password you think was exposed straight away.
