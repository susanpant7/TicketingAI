# Printers and Scanning

## Adding a printer
Office printers are shared and managed on the print server. On a company laptop, open Software Center and install "Office Printers", or add the printer from Settings > Printers using the address \\print.ticket.com. Choose the printer on your floor, which is named with the floor number, for example "Floor3-Color".

## Secure print release
Print jobs are held until you release them with your access card. Send the job to the printer, walk to the printer, tap your access card, and choose the job to print. Jobs not released within 24 hours are deleted automatically.

## Printer is not printing
Check that the printer is on and shows no error. Make sure your job appears in the queue and that you released it with your card. If the job is stuck, cancel it in the print queue and send it again. If a paper jam or an error is shown on the panel, tell IT with the printer name instead of repeated attempts.

## Paper jams and toner
For a paper jam, follow the panel instructions and remove the paper gently. Do not pull hard or use tools. Toner and paper are stocked in the supply cupboard on each floor. If the cupboard is empty, raise a facilities ticket.

## Scanning to email
Use the scan function on the printer panel, tap your access card, and choose "Scan to Me". The scanned PDF arrives in your company email within a minute. Scans are limited to 25 MB per document.

## Printing from home
Printing from home to office printers is not supported. If you need a printed document at home, you can print on your own printer, but company confidential documents should not be printed outside the office.
