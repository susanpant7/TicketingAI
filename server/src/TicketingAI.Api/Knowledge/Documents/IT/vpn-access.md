# VPN Access

## When you need the VPN
You must connect to the company VPN whenever you access internal systems from outside the office, such as the HR portal, internal wikis, file shares and the ticketing system. You do not need the VPN inside the office network. Email and chat work without the VPN.

## Installing the VPN client
The VPN client is FortiClient, and it is pre-installed on company laptops. If it is missing, open Software Center on your laptop and install "FortiClient VPN". The VPN gateway address is vpn.ticket.com on port 443.

## Connecting to the VPN
Open FortiClient, select the "Company VPN" profile, and enter your company username and password. Approve the multi-factor authentication prompt on your phone when it appears. When connected, the client shows a green status and the connection time.

## VPN connection fails or hangs
If the VPN will not connect, first check that your home internet works by opening any website. Then close FortiClient completely and reopen it, and try again. If it hangs at 40 percent or 98 percent, disconnect from Wi-Fi, reconnect, and retry, or switch to a mobile hotspot to find out whether your home network blocks it.

## VPN says username or password is wrong
Your VPN login is the same as your company login. If it says the password is wrong, your password may have expired or been changed recently. Reset it using the self-service password reset page, then try the VPN again. After 5 failed attempts your account is locked for 15 minutes.

## VPN did not send the authentication prompt
If you do not receive the multi-factor prompt, check that your phone has internet access and open the Microsoft Authenticator app manually. Make sure the phone time is set to automatic, because wrong time settings break the codes. If it still fails, raise a ticket with IT.

## Slow VPN connection
The VPN sends only internal traffic through the company network, so general web browsing should not slow down. If internal sites are slow, disconnect and reconnect to get a closer gateway, close heavy downloads, and use a wired or 5 GHz Wi-Fi connection. Report persistent slowness to IT with the time and the site you tried.

## VPN on mobile phones
The VPN is supported on company laptops. Phone access is limited to email, calendar and chat through the approved mobile apps.
