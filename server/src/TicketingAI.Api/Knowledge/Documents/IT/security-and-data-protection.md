department: IT

## Protecting your laptop
Lock your screen with Windows+L whenever you leave your desk, and never leave your laptop unattended in public places or in a car. Screens lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Keep disk encryption and endpoint protection on.

## Storing company files
Store work files in OneDrive and in the approved team sites, not on your desktop or on personal cloud accounts. These locations are backed up and can be recovered if the laptop fails. Do not email company files to your personal email address.

## USB drives and removable media
USB storage devices are blocked on company laptops by default. If you need to transfer a file, use OneDrive or ask IT to enable a USB device for a specific task with your manager's approval. Unknown USB devices found in the office must be handed to IT and never plugged in.

## Reporting a security incident
Report any lost device, suspected account compromise, phishing click or data leak to IT immediately at it-help@ticket.com or on extension 100, and do not try to fix it yourself. Early reporting limits the damage, and employees who report in good faith are not blamed.

## Classifying data
Company data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Employee records, salary data and customer data are Confidential or Restricted, and are shared only with people who need them for their work. When unsure, treat the data as Confidential.

## Working in public places
When working in cafes or while travelling, use the VPN, avoid public Wi-Fi without it, and use a privacy screen if you handle Confidential data. Do not discuss confidential matters on calls where others can hear you.

## Visitors and access cards
Visitors must sign in at reception and wear a visitor badge, and employees must accompany them in the office. Never lend your access card to anyone. Report a lost access card to reception and IT immediately so it can be disabled.
