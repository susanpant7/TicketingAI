# Email and Calendar

## Email setup
Company email runs on Outlook with Microsoft 365. On a company laptop, Outlook is already set up with your company account. On the web, you can sign in at outlook.office.com from anywhere using your company login and multi-factor authentication.

## Mailbox size and attachments
Each mailbox is 50 GB. The maximum size of one email, including attachments, is 25 MB. For larger files, share a link from OneDrive instead of attaching the file.

## Mailbox is full
If you cannot send or receive email because the mailbox is full, delete large attachments and empty the Deleted Items and Junk folders. You can also move old emails into an archive. If the mailbox is still full, raise a ticket and IT can increase the quota temporarily.

## Email on your phone
To read company email on your phone, install the Outlook app and enrol the device in mobile device management when asked. Enrolment lets IT wipe only company data if the phone is lost. If email stops syncing after a password change, remove the account in the app and add it again.

## Out-of-office replies
Set an out-of-office reply in Outlook under File > Automatic Replies, with the dates and the name of a colleague who can help. Set it before any leave of a full working day or more. You can also set it in Outlook on the web from the settings menu.

## Shared mailboxes and distribution lists
Teams that work from a shared address, such as support@ticket.com, use a shared mailbox. To get access to a shared mailbox or distribution list, raise a ticket with your manager's approval. Access is added within 1 working day.

## Calendar and meeting rooms
Book meeting rooms by adding the room as a resource in your Outlook meeting invite. You can share your calendar with colleagues from Outlook calendar settings. Teams meeting links are added automatically when you choose "Teams Meeting" in the invite.

## Phishing and suspicious emails
Do not click links or open attachments in emails you did not expect, especially those that ask for passwords, payments or urgent action. Use the "Report phishing" button in Outlook to report suspicious emails, and do not forward them. If you clicked a link or entered your password, tell IT immediately and change your password.
