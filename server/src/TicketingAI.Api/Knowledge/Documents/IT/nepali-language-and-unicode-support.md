## Typing in Nepali on your laptop
Company laptops support typing in Nepali (Devanagari). Open Settings > Time & language > Language & region, add "Nepali (Nepal)", and choose a keyboard layout, such as Nepali Romanized or Nepali Traditional. Switch between English and Nepali with the Windows key and the space bar, or from the language icon in the taskbar.

## Unicode, not legacy fonts
Always use Unicode fonts for Nepali, such as Mangal or Noto Sans Devanagari. Legacy fonts such as Preeti look like Nepali only when the font is installed, and show strange English characters in email, chat and search. Documents in Unicode can be searched, copied and read by everyone on any device.

## Converting Preeti text to Unicode
If you have a document written in Preeti or another legacy font, use the Preeti to Unicode converter on the intranet under IT > Tools. Paste the text, click Convert, and copy the Unicode result into your document. Check the result for spelling and conjunct letters, because conversions are not always exact.

## Nepali in Outlook, Teams and Word
Nepali works in Outlook subjects and bodies, Teams chat and Word documents. If the characters show as boxes, change the font to Mangal or Noto Sans Devanagari. When you print or export to PDF, choose a Unicode font to avoid broken letters.

## Nepali on your phone
Both Android and iPhone support a Nepali keyboard. Add it in the phone's keyboard settings. The Outlook and Teams mobile apps accept Nepali text with the system keyboard.

## BS dates and the Nepali calendar
The BS date converter on the intranet changes dates between Bikram Sambat and the English calendar. You can also add the BS calendar to Outlook from the intranet so that Nepali holidays show in your calendar. If a date looks wrong in a document, check whether it is BS or English.

## Fonts for designers and publications
Designers who work with Nepali typefaces can ask IT for approved fonts and the Adobe language settings through a software request. Fonts must be properly licensed. Do not install fonts downloaded from unknown websites.

## Getting help
If Nepali typing or fonts do not work, raise a ticket with IT and say which application and which font you use. IT can reset the language settings and install missing fonts.
