# Power Backup and Internet Outages

## Power cuts at the office
The office has an uninterruptible power supply (UPS) and a backup generator. When the electricity goes off, the lights, servers, network and Wi-Fi stay on, and the generator starts within about a minute. Your laptop's battery covers any short gap, so you do not need to do anything.

## Office internet backup
The office has two internet connections from different providers, and traffic switches to the second connection automatically if the first one fails. If the internet is down for everyone in the office, IT already knows. Tell IT only if it has been more than 15 minutes.

## Power cuts at home
Keep your laptop charged, since the battery lasts a few hours. If the power goes off at home for a long time, move to a place with power, or come to the office if it is open. Home power backup, such as an inverter, is your own responsibility. The remote work policy requires a reliable setup.

## Internet down at home
If your home internet stops, first restart your router. If it does not come back within 10 minutes, share the mobile data from your phone as a hotspot from Ncell or Nepal Telecom, and connect your laptop to it. Using the VPN over mobile data works, but video calls can use a lot of data.

## Mobile data costs
The company reimburses mobile data for work during a home internet outage, up to NPR 500 per month, with a screenshot of the data pack or a receipt. This is on top of the home internet allowance in the remote work policy.

## When to switch to the office
If your home internet or power is down for more than half a day, tell your manager and work from the office. You can also use the office on remote days for this reason without asking for approval in advance.

## Load-shedding and planned outages
The Nepal Electricity Authority announces planned outages for some areas. Check the announcement for your area, and plan meetings and calls around it. Tell your manager if your area has long outages, and the team will arrange a better schedule.

## Reporting problems to IT
Report an outage that affects the office network, VPN or company systems to IT at it-help@ticket.com or on extension 100, with the time it started. IT cannot repair problems at home, but can help you check that the problem is not on the company side.
