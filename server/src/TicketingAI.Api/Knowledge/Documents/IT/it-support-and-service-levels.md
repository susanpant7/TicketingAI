## How to contact IT support
You can reach IT support by email at it-help@ticket.com, by phone on extension 100, or by raising a ticket in the ticketing system. Raising a ticket is the preferred way, because it is tracked and prioritized. Walk-ins are welcome at the IT desk on the ground floor.

## Support hours
IT support is available from 08:00 to 20:00, Sunday to Friday. Outside those hours and on public holidays, only critical issues are handled by the on-call engineer, whose number is on the intranet. Non-critical requests wait until the next working day.

## Ticket priorities and response times
Tickets are prioritized by impact. P1 Critical, such as an outage affecting many people, gets a response within 1 hour. P2 High, such as one person unable to work, gets a response within 4 hours. P3 Normal, such as a problem with a workaround, gets a response within 1 working day. P4 Requests, such as new software or equipment, get a response within 3 working days.

## What to include in a ticket
Describe what you were doing, what you expected, and what actually happened. Include the exact error message or a screenshot, the time it started, and whether it affects others. Say how urgent it is and whether you are working remotely. A complete description gets faster help.

## Raising a ticket for another person
Managers can raise tickets for their team members, for example for a new joiner's laptop. Put the affected person's name and department in the ticket, and tell them so IT can reach them.

## What IT does not support
IT supports company devices and company systems only. IT cannot repair personal laptops, phones or home networks, and does not install personal software. Linux laptops are not part of the standard setup.

## Escalating a ticket
If your ticket has not been answered within its target time, reply to the ticket email and write "Escalation needed", or contact your manager. Escalations go to the IT team lead, who reviews them the same day.
