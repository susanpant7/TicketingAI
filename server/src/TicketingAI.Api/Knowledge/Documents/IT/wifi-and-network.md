## Office Wi-Fi for employees
Employees connect to the "Corp-WiFi" network using their company email and password. The first connection asks you to accept a certificate, which is expected. Your laptop remembers the network afterwards. Do not connect company laptops to guest Wi-Fi.

## Guest Wi-Fi for visitors
Visitors use the "Guest-WiFi" network. The password is shown at reception and changes every week. Guest Wi-Fi gives internet access only, and it cannot reach internal systems.

## Cannot connect to Corp-WiFi
If you cannot connect, first check that you did not change your password recently, since your saved Wi-Fi login may be outdated. Select "Forget this network" in your Wi-Fi settings and connect again with your current credentials. If it still fails, check that your account is not locked, and raise a ticket.

## Wi-Fi is slow or drops
Move closer to an access point and use the 5 GHz band if your laptop offers it. Restarting the Wi-Fi adapter or the laptop often fixes dropouts. If many people on one floor are affected, report it to IT with the floor and the time, because it may be an access point problem.

## Wired network
Network ports are available at meeting rooms and at desks on some floors. Plug in a standard network cable, and the laptop connects automatically. Wired is the better choice for video calls and large downloads.

## Home internet problems
IT cannot repair your home internet, but can help check whether the problem is on the company side. Test another website and another device first. If other websites work and only company systems do not, connect to the VPN and raise a ticket.
