department: Finance

## Why finance deadlines matter
Finance closes its books every month, and anything submitted after a deadline is recorded in the following month. This affects when you are reimbursed and how budgets look. Plan to submit everything before the deadlines below. All dates in this document refer to Nepali (BS) calendar months.

## Expense claim cutoff
Expense claims approved by your manager before the 20th of the month are paid with that month's salary, on the last working day. Claims approved after the 20th are paid with the following month's salary. Submit claims within 30 days of the expense in any case.

## Vendor invoice cutoff
Invoices should reach accounts payable by the 25th of the month to be included in that month's accruals. Requesters must confirm goods and services received by the last working day of the month. Late confirmation delays payment to the vendor.

## Corporate card reconciliation
Corporate card transactions for the previous month must be reconciled by the 5th of the following month. Finance sends reminders on the 3rd and 5th.

## Month-end close
Finance closes the books by the 5th working day after the end of the month. During the close, finance may ask you questions about expenses or invoices, and expects answers within 1 working day.

## Year-end close
The Nepali fiscal year ends at the end of Ashadh. In the last week of Ashadh, finance publishes a year-end deadline list with earlier cutoffs than usual, since the closing is more detailed. Purchase requests for the old year must be approved by 15 Ashadh.

## Public holidays and Saturdays
When a deadline falls on a Saturday or public holiday, it moves to the working day before it. The finance calendar with all dates is published on the intranet each Shrawan.
