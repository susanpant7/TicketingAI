# VAT Bills, PAN Bills and TDS

## Two kinds of bills in Nepal
Vendors in Nepal issue either a VAT bill or a PAN bill. A VAT bill comes from a VAT-registered vendor, and shows the VAT of 13 percent as a separate amount. A PAN bill comes from a vendor who is registered for PAN but not for VAT, and has no VAT. Both must show the vendor's PAN or VAT number.

## What a valid bill must show
A valid bill shows the vendor's name, address and PAN or VAT number, the bill number and date, the buyer's name, what was bought, the amount, and the VAT if any. The buyer's name must be the company name, "Ticketing AI Pvt. Ltd.", and the company PAN. Handwritten or photocopied bills, and bills without a PAN number, are not accepted for company payments.

## Electronic billing
Registered businesses issue bills from billing software approved by the Inland Revenue Department. If a vendor gives you a bill from a notebook or a plain printout, ask for a proper bill. Small shops may only have a simple PAN bill, which is fine when it shows the PAN.

## Why bills matter
The company can claim VAT back and deduct the cost from its taxable income only when it holds a valid bill in its name. A claim without a proper bill can be rejected, because finance can lose the tax benefit. This is why the expense policy requires a VAT or PAN bill for expenses above NPR 1,000.

## Vendors without a PAN
Finance does not set up vendors without a PAN, except for very small cash purchases through petty cash. If a vendor refuses to give a PAN bill, choose another vendor where possible, and tell finance about it. Vendors who are individuals, such as freelancers, must also have a PAN.

## TDS on vendor payments
Finance withholds tax at source (TDS) on many vendor payments, such as services, rent, commissions and contracts, and deposits it with the IRD. The rate depends on the type of payment, on whether the vendor is VAT registered, and on the current tax rules. The vendor receives the amount after TDS, with a TDS certificate on request.

## Vendors who ask for the full amount
If a vendor asks for the full amount without TDS, do not agree to it. Tell them that the company must withhold tax by law. Finance can explain the calculation to the vendor, and send the TDS details.

## Questions
Send bills that you are unsure about to ap@ticket.com before you pay or commit. Finance replies within 2 working days, and can tell you whether a bill is acceptable.
