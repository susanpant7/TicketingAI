## Who can have a corporate card
Corporate credit cards are issued to managers and to employees who travel or buy for the company often. Ask your manager to request a card for you through the finance portal under Cards > New Card Request. Cards are issued within 10 working days after approval by your department head and finance.

## Card limits
The default monthly limit is NPR 300,000 per card. A higher temporary limit, for example for a conference or large trip, can be requested from finance at least 5 working days ahead with your manager's approval. Limits cannot be exceeded.

## What the card can be used for
Use the corporate card only for approved business expenses, such as travel, accommodation, client meals and approved subscriptions. Follow the same limits as in the expense policy, for example NPR 8,000 per hotel night. Cash withdrawals are not allowed. Personal purchases on the corporate card are never allowed, even if you plan to repay them.

## Reconciling card transactions
By the 5th of each month, you must reconcile the previous month's card transactions in the finance portal. Match each transaction with a receipt, choose a category and cost center, and submit it to your manager. Missing receipts need a missing receipt declaration, approved by your manager.

## Late reconciliation
If transactions are not reconciled by the deadline, finance sends a reminder. After a second reminder the card is suspended until the transactions are reconciled. Unreconciled personal charges can be deducted from your salary.

## Lost or stolen card
Call the bank's card helpline on the back of the card immediately to block it, and then inform finance at finance@ticket.com. Do not wait until you are back in the office. A replacement card is issued within 5 working days.

## Returning the card
Return your corporate card to finance when you leave the company or change to a role that does not need it. Finance cancels the card and checks that all transactions are reconciled before your final salary is paid.
