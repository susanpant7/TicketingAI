## Where vendors send invoices
Vendors must send invoices to ap@ticket.com, the accounts payable mailbox, and not to individual employees. Every invoice must show the purchase order number, the company name and PAN, the invoice date, the amount and tax, and the vendor's bank details. If you receive an invoice directly, forward it to ap@ticket.com the same day.

## Payment terms
The standard payment term is 30 days from the date of a correct invoice. Vendors are paid in the weekly payment run every Thursday. An invoice approved by Tuesday is normally paid in that Thursday's run if it is already due.

## Three-way match
Finance pays an invoice only when three things match: the purchase order, the goods received confirmation, and the invoice. If the amounts differ, finance contacts the requester to resolve the difference before payment. Missing receipt confirmation is the most common cause of delayed payments.

## Adding a new vendor
New vendors must be set up before they can be paid. Ask the vendor to fill in the vendor registration form with their bank details, PAN and contact person, and send it to ap@ticket.com. Finance verifies the bank details by calling the vendor, which takes up to 5 working days. Never change a vendor's bank details based on an email request alone.

## Urgent payments
If a vendor must be paid outside the weekly run, submit an urgent payment request in the finance portal with the reason and the invoice. It needs approval from your department head and finance manager. Urgent payments are processed within 2 working days and should be rare.

## Checking the status of a payment
To check whether an invoice was paid, look in the finance portal under Payments > Vendor Invoices, which shows each invoice's status. For questions that the portal does not answer, email ap@ticket.com with the invoice number and vendor name. Accounts payable replies within 2 working days.

## Disputed or duplicate invoices
If an invoice looks wrong or appears twice, tell accounts payable immediately and do not approve it. Finance will place the invoice on hold and contact the vendor. Never ask a vendor to reissue an invoice with a different date to meet a deadline.
