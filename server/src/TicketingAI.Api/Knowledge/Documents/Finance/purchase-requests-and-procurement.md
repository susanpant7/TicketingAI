## When you need a purchase request
Raise a purchase request for any purchase of goods or services above NPR 10,000 that is paid by the company, such as equipment, software subscriptions, consultants and event costs. Purchases of NPR 10,000 or less can be paid by the employee and claimed as an expense. Never order or commit to a vendor before the purchase request is approved.

## How to raise a purchase request
Submit the request in the finance portal under Procurement > New Purchase Request. Include what you need, why you need it, the vendor, the quote or price, the cost center and the delivery date. Attach the vendor quote as a PDF.

## Approval limits for purchases
Purchases up to NPR 100,000 are approved by your manager. Purchases above NPR 100,000 and up to NPR 1,000,000 need your department head as well. Purchases above NPR 1,000,000 also need approval from the CFO. The request moves through approvers automatically in the portal.

## Quotes for larger purchases
For purchases above NPR 500,000, attach at least 3 quotes from different vendors, or explain why only one vendor is possible. Finance can ask you to negotiate before approving. Recurring subscriptions are reviewed again each year.

## Purchase orders
After approval, finance issues a purchase order (PO) with a PO number within 2 working days. Give the PO number to the vendor. Invoices without a valid PO number are returned to the vendor, and the payment is delayed.

## Receiving goods and services
When the goods arrive or the service is done, confirm receipt in the finance portal under Procurement > Goods Received. Finance only pays the invoice after receipt is confirmed. Report damaged or missing items to the vendor and to finance within 5 working days.

## Software and IT equipment
Purchases of software and IT equipment also need an IT review for security and compatibility. Raise the request through IT first, and IT will attach its approval to the purchase request. Do not buy software with a personal or company card without this review.

## Urgent purchases
If something is urgently needed, mark the request as urgent and explain the business impact, and tell your approvers directly. Urgent requests are processed within 1 working day. An urgent request does not remove the need for approval.
