# Nepali Fiscal Year and Statutory Deadlines

## The Nepali fiscal year
The company uses the Nepali fiscal year, which starts on 1 Shrawan and ends on the last day of Ashadh. This is roughly mid-July to mid-July in the English calendar. Budgets, accounts and tax are all based on this year, and finance works in Bikram Sambat (BS) dates.

## Quarters
The fiscal year has four quarters: Shrawan to Ashwin, Kartik to Poush, Magh to Chaitra, and Baisakh to Ashadh. Finance reviews budgets after each quarter closes. The fourth quarter is the busiest, because it includes the year-end close and the tax filing preparation.

## BS and English dates
Policies and the finance portal show BS dates, with the English date next to them where helpful. The BS months are Baisakh, Jestha, Ashadh, Shrawan, Bhadra, Ashwin, Kartik, Mangsir, Poush, Magh, Falgun and Chaitra. A BS month starts around the middle of an English month, so cutoffs such as the 20th are counted in BS months. If you are unsure, use the date shown in the portal.

## Monthly VAT and TDS filing
Finance files the monthly VAT return and deposits the TDS it has withheld with the Inland Revenue Department (IRD), normally by the 25th of the following BS month. Employees do not file these, but must give finance their bills and invoices on time, so they can be included in the right month.

## Annual income tax return
The company files its annual income tax return with the IRD after the fiscal year ends, normally within 3 months, which is by the end of Ashwin. Finance prepares it with the audited accounts. Finance may ask department heads for supporting documents in Shrawan and Bhadra, and expects answers within 2 working days.

## Statutory audit
The company's accounts are audited every year by a registered auditor. The audit mostly happens in Shrawan and Bhadra, after the year-end close. Auditors may ask employees to explain transactions, and employees must cooperate and provide original documents.

## SSF and other monthly payments
Finance deposits SSF contributions each month, using the payroll figures. Payroll also files the monthly employee TDS. These filings follow the deadlines set by the SSF and the IRD, which can change, and finance confirms the current dates each year.

## Penalties for late filing
Late filing and late payment of tax can lead to penalties and interest, which are costs for the company. This is why finance deadlines are strict. If you cannot meet a deadline, tell finance before it passes and not after.
