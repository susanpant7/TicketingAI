## Tax invoices
Every business expense claim above NPR 1,000 needs a receipt, and purchases from vendors need a proper tax invoice showing the company name, the company PAN, the vendor's PAN, the date, the amount and the tax. Ask the vendor for the company details before you pay. Invoices that do not show these details may not be accepted for reimbursement.

## Company details for invoices
Ask vendors to invoice "Ticketing AI Pvt. Ltd." with the PAN shown in the finance portal under Help > Company Details. Do not use your personal name on business invoices. If a vendor made an invoice in the wrong name, ask for a corrected invoice before submitting it.

## Tax on purchases
VAT (13 percent in Nepal) paid on business purchases can usually be recovered by the company only when there is a valid tax invoice. Always keep the original invoice, not just the card receipt. Finance decides whether tax can be recovered.

## Withholding tax on vendor payments
Some vendor payments, such as services from individuals and consultants, require tax (TDS) to be withheld before payment. Finance handles this automatically when vendors are set up with the right tax category. Tell finance if a vendor is tax exempt, and send their exemption certificate.

## Gifts and entertainment
Gifts and entertainment given to clients must be recorded in the finance portal with the name, company and amount. Gifts above NPR 5,000 per person need the department head's approval. Gifts to government officials are not allowed.

## Record keeping
Finance keeps invoices, contracts and payment records for 7 years. Employees must keep original receipts for expense claims until the claim is paid. Never destroy finance documents without checking with finance.

## Audits
Internal and external auditors review finance records every year. If an auditor contacts you, answer promptly and honestly, and tell finance. Documents asked for by auditors must be provided in original form, and must not be changed.

## Employee income tax
Employee income tax is handled by payroll, not by the finance team. For tax declarations and deductions, see the payroll and salary policy, and email payroll@ticket.com for questions.
