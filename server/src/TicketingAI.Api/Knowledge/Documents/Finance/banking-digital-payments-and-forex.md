# Banking, Digital Payments and Foreign Currency

## How the company pays
The company pays vendors, salaries and reimbursements by bank transfer, using the Nepal banking system, for example through connectIPS or the bank's own transfer service. Large payments must always go through a bank. Finance does not make cash payments except from petty cash, within its limits.

## Digital wallets and QR payments
For small business expenses, you can pay with a digital wallet or a QR code, such as eSewa, Khalti or Fonepay. For reimbursement, keep a screenshot of the payment showing the merchant name, the amount, the date and the transaction ID, and a VAT or PAN bill from the merchant. Payments without a bill are not reimbursed above NPR 1,000.

## Bank details of employees
Employees must have a bank account in their own name for salary and reimbursements. Changes to your account are requested in the HR portal and are described in the payroll policy. Finance never pays reimbursements into another person's account.

## Paying vendors abroad
Payments to vendors outside Nepal, such as for software subscriptions and international services, are paid in foreign currency through the company's bank. They follow Nepal Rastra Bank (NRB) foreign exchange rules, and the bank can ask for documents such as the invoice and the contract. Allow 2 to 3 weeks for the first payment to a new foreign vendor.

## Do not use personal cards for foreign payments
Nepali-issued cards generally have strict limits on international payments. Do not pay foreign subscriptions with your personal card and claim them back, since this is often not possible or reimbursable. Raise a purchase request, and finance will arrange the payment through the bank.

## The corporate card abroad
The corporate card is for payments in Nepal. International use is limited by the bank and by NRB rules, and needs to be enabled before a trip. Ask finance at least 10 working days before your trip abroad.

## Exchange rates
Finance converts foreign currency amounts at the Nepal Rastra Bank exchange rate on the date of the transaction. The rate shown in the finance portal is the one used for accounts. Differences between the payment amount and the booked amount are recorded by finance.

## Suspicious payment requests
Be careful with emails or calls that ask you to send money, change bank details or use a mobile wallet urgently. Always confirm payment requests by calling a known number, and tell finance and IT at once if you think it is fraud.
