## What petty cash is for
Petty cash covers small, urgent office expenses that cannot be paid by card or invoice, such as courier fees, stationery or refreshments for a visitor. Each purchase is limited to NPR 5,000. Larger amounts need a purchase request or an expense claim.

## Getting petty cash
Collect petty cash from the finance desk on the second floor between 10:00 and 16:00 on working days. Fill in a petty cash voucher with the amount, purpose and cost center, and get your manager's signature. Finance records the amount against your name.

## Settling petty cash
Return the receipt and any change to the finance desk within 3 working days of the purchase. Finance closes the voucher when receipts and change match the amount issued. Unsettled petty cash is reported to your manager.

## Cash advances for travel
Employees travelling for more than 3 days can request a cash advance of up to 70 percent of the estimated travel cost. Request it at least 5 working days before the trip, with your manager's approval, in the finance portal under Cash > Advance Request. The advance is paid to your bank account.

## Settling a travel advance
Settle the advance within 10 days after returning, by submitting an expense claim with all receipts. If the advance is more than what you spent, return the difference to finance. If you spent more, the difference is reimbursed with the claim. Unsettled advances are deducted from your next salary.

## Other advances
Finance does not give cash advances for personal needs. A salary advance is a different request and is handled by HR and payroll, not by finance.
