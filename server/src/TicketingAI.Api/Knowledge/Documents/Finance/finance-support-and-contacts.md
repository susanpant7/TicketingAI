# Finance Support and Contacts

## Who to contact in finance
For general finance questions, email finance@ticket.com. For vendor invoices and payments, email ap@ticket.com. For customer invoices and collections, email billing@ticket.com. For salary and payslip questions, email payroll@ticket.com, which is handled with HR.

## Finance support hours
The finance team is available from 09:00 to 17:00, Sunday to Friday. The finance desk on the second floor is open from 10:00 to 16:00 for petty cash and receipts. Emails are answered within 2 working days.

## The finance portal
Most finance requests are made in the finance portal, including purchase requests, cards, budgets, payments and billing. Sign in with your company login and multi-factor authentication. The portal needs the VPN when you work outside the office.

## Which team handles what
Expense claims are submitted in the HR portal and follow the expense reimbursement policy, with finance doing the final check before payment. Salary, payslips and tax declarations belong to payroll. Purchases, vendor payments, corporate cards, budgets and customer invoices belong to finance.

## Raising a finance ticket
If your question is not answered by the portal or the email contacts, raise a ticket with the category "Finance" in the ticketing system. Describe what you need, include the invoice, purchase order or claim number, and say how urgent it is. Finance responds within 2 working days.

## Escalation
If you have not received an answer within the expected time, reply to the same email and write "Escalation needed", or ask your manager to contact the finance manager. The finance manager reviews escalations within 1 working day.

## Fraud and suspicious requests
Be careful with emails that ask you to change bank details, pay urgently or buy gift cards, even when they appear to come from a manager. Do not act on them. Report them immediately to finance@ticket.com and to IT.
