# Customer Invoicing and Collections

## Requesting a customer invoice
To invoice a customer, submit an invoice request in the finance portal under Billing > New Invoice or email billing@ticket.com. Include the customer name and billing address, the customer's PO number if they gave one, what was delivered, the amount and the agreed payment terms. Finance issues the invoice within 2 working days.

## Standard payment terms
Customers pay within 30 days of the invoice date unless the contract says otherwise. Different terms need approval from the finance manager before they are promised to a customer. Do not agree payment terms in an email without checking.

## Where customers pay
Customers pay by bank transfer to the company account shown on the invoice. Cheques and cash are not accepted. Never ask a customer to pay into any account that is not on the invoice, including a personal account.

## Following up overdue invoices
Finance sends a reminder when an invoice is 7 days overdue and a second reminder after 21 days. Account managers are asked to contact the customer when an invoice is more than 30 days overdue. Work with finance and do not offer discounts or extensions yourself.

## Credit notes and corrections
If an invoice has a mistake, ask finance for a credit note and a corrected invoice, and do not ask the customer to ignore it. Credit notes above NPR 100,000 need approval from the department head. Finance sends both documents to the customer.

## Customer disputes
If a customer disputes an invoice, tell finance and the account manager the same day. Finance puts the invoice on hold from reminders while the dispute is reviewed, and the account manager gathers the details. Disputes should be resolved within 15 working days.

## Foreign currency invoices
Invoices in a currency other than NPR must be agreed with finance before they are issued. Finance converts at the Nepal Rastra Bank exchange rate on the invoice date, and exchange differences are recorded by finance.
