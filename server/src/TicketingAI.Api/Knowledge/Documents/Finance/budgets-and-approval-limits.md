department: Finance

## Financial year and budget cycle
The financial year follows the Nepali fiscal year and runs from 1 Shrawan to the end of Ashadh (mid-July to mid-July). Annual budget planning takes place in Baisakh and Jestha, and department heads submit budget proposals to finance by 15 Jestha. The final budgets are approved by the CFO in Ashadh and apply from 1 Shrawan.

## Budget owners
Each department has a budget owner, usually the department head, who is responsible for spending within the approved budget. Managers can see their own cost center's budget and spending in the finance portal under Budgets > My Cost Center. Ask your budget owner if you are not sure whether there is budget for something.

## Spending approval limits
Spending up to NPR 100,000 can be approved by a manager. Spending above NPR 100,000 and up to NPR 1,000,000 needs the department head's approval. Spending above NPR 1,000,000 also needs the CFO's approval. These limits apply to purchase requests, contracts and one-off payments, and cannot be split into smaller parts to avoid approval.

## Contracts and signing authority
Only the CFO and the CEO can sign contracts on behalf of the company. Employees cannot sign contracts with vendors or customers, even for small amounts. Send contracts to legal and finance for review before any signature.

## Budget transfers
If a cost center needs more budget, the budget owner can request a transfer from another cost center in the finance portal. Transfers within a department are approved by the department head and finance. Transfers between departments also need the CFO's approval.

## Overspending
If a cost center is forecast to exceed its budget, the budget owner must tell finance as soon as it is known. Overspending of more than 10 percent of the annual budget needs the CFO's approval. Finance does not pay for new purchase requests once the budget is exhausted until the owner explains it.

## Quarterly budget reviews
Finance reviews actual spending against budget every quarter, shortly after the quarter closes. Budget owners meet finance to explain differences above 10 percent and to update forecasts. Unused budget does not carry over into the next year.
